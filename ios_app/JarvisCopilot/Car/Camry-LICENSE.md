# Camry model

`Camry.bin` is baked by `ios_app/scripts/car/bake_car.py` from:

This work is based on "2025 Toyota Camry (XV80) Hybrid"
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2025-toyota-camry-xv80-hybrid-53f8cbf2483940fba956f4e740a3a4ae)
by Ddiaz Design (https://sketchfab.com/ddiaz-design), licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Changes: geometry simplified (1.23 M → ~218 k triangles), textures and decals removed, parts
regrouped by material, the paint recoloured to Toyota Dark Cosmos (8Z3). The baked mesh is shared
under the same licence: credit the author, no commercial use, same licence for derivatives.
