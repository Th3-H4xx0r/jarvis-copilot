# VeSync ESF551 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Integrate the VeSync ESF551 scale with live BLE readings, local health history, a detailed screen, and JarvisCopilot controls.

**Architecture:** `ScaleProtocol` decodes the reverse-engineered VsV2 traffic, while `ScaleManager` owns CoreBluetooth. The existing `ScaleHistoryStore` persists normalized readings and profiles; a `WearableDevice` adapter publishes safe operations to Jarvis.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6, SwiftUI, CoreBluetooth, XCTest, iOS 26/macOS.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-01-vesync-esf551-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Support the physical Etekcity ESF551 family only until other families are independently verified.
- Store mass in kilograms and convert only for display.
- Reject malformed, partial, or checksum-invalid BLE frames.
- Do not send unverified BLE controls.
- Keep health metrics local and label them estimates.
