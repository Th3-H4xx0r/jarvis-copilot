# 2026-10-16 — iPhone battery, round 4 (measure first)

He said: "It shows 40% drain for 17h 37m background and 1h 2m foreground. The background usage is especially
draining the most I believe, do deep analysis and see what else can be optimized". His pick on the keepalive:
**measure first** — ship the waste fixes and better measurement now, decide the keepalive after a day of numbers.

Found (hourly probe + server logs, Oct 15 20:00 → Oct 16 11:00 CDT):
- Background CPU is small: ~20–25 CPU-s/h in quiet hours (3–8 am). ~80 % of the app's CPU (≈1,650 of 2,080 s)
  went into the 5 hours with screen use — main thread, SwiftUI's renderer, SceneKit (3D models).
- Running all day in the background: the silent-audio keepalive (83 % of background time in the Oct 15 MetricKit
  day), the bridge socket (server ping every 40 s + the phone's pong ≈ 90 round trips/h; Cloudflare reaps idle
  sockets at ~51 s), and the band's phone-managed measuring (held BLE link + phone-timed readings).
- Waste: a widget refresh = 11 requests (7 health histories, chat list, open chat, coding sessions, agent
  sessions); any coding change on the Mac sends `widgets {"what":["coding"]}` every 30 min and the phone refetches
  everything (~22 requests/h overnight, half its background traffic). Health days re-uploaded in bursts of 17–18
  POSTs (~38 KB each), 5 times overnight. Keepalive lost 10:38 pm–12:45 am (18 sessions of ~30 s; 2 Mac → phone
  invokes failed 502) and only re-armed when the app was opened. No Low Power Mode handling.

- [x] **1. Widgets refetch only what changed.** A `widgets` push refreshes only the areas in `what`; in the
  background the server's part refreshes at most hourly, plus when the phone is unlocked.
  - Built: `WidgetDataProvider.area`; a push refreshes only its `what`; background gate 1 h per area, unlock
    fetches what changed (30 min for unpushed areas). 7 new WidgetDataHubTests ✓.

- [x] **2. Health uploads send only days whose content changed.**
  - Built: `DayPushLedger` (HealthDayPayload.swift) — fingerprint without `synced_at` (and battery on days
    before yesterday), resent after 24 h. 6 DayPushLedgerTests ✓.

- [x] **3. Keepalive heals itself on any background wake; stops in Low Power Mode.**
  - Built: `sync(active:)` restarts a dead engine (every connect/reconnect/BG refresh); Low Power Mode releases it
    and closes a backgrounded socket so invokes go by push. Keepalive test ✓.
