- [ ] **1. Phone voice: noises interrupt Jarvis.** A bang on the table, a
  cough, and sometimes smaller noises cut the reply off with nothing said.
  Only talking (the start of a new request) should interrupt.
  - Found: the phone interrupts on loudness alone — 300 ms of sound over a
    low bar (0.012, the same bar as quiet speech) within 500 ms. Nothing asks
    whether it is a voice, so a cough or a bang with its echo clears it.
  - Tried on the Mac with test clips: Apple's sound classifier can't tell,
    because Jarvis's own echo under the reply already scores "speech" 0.6–0.95.
    The on-device transcriber can, but only with the reply turned down: at
    full volume it turns the echo into words ("see", "tell", "monkey") right
    where a noise lands.
  - ✅ Built: a loud sound turns the reply down to 20% and opens a words
    check (Apple's on-device transcriber, nothing leaves the phone). Words that
    aren't the reply's own → interrupt (~0.9 s after you start); your first
    words carry into the new turn. No words (or only "uh"/"hmm") → the volume
    comes back after ~1.2 s. Noise that goes on 4 s is let go. Replayed on the
    Mac: 13/13 noises (bangs, knocks, coughs, typing, clinks) gave no words;
    all 7 speech clips were heard (0.6 s, 1.5 s whispered).
  - ✅ iOS voice tests 144/144 (new: noise dips only, cough filler, echo words,
    long noise, no-recogniser fallback, on-device hand-over); all 360 voice
