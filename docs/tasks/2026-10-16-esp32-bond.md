# 2026-10-16 — ESP32 notification relay keeps dropping (firmware 1.3)

He said: "it just connects for a sec, then disconnects and shows this [Failed to encrypt the connection, the
connection has timed out unexpectedly], and if I forget the device and connect again, it works for a few sec, then
disconnects again".

Found (serial log + NVS dump of the board, 14:53–14:55 CDT):
- The board's live bond store held **no bonds**; BondKeeper's copy (`jc_bt_copy`) held one, filed under
  `75:27:29:be:72:a6` (AddrType 1, a resolvable private address); `jc_bonds/heals` = 3, so the board had stopped
  healing: `[bond] a saved bond is gone again; waiting for the phone instead of rebooting` at every boot.
- Bluedroid files a bond under the address the phone paired from, and an iPhone pairs from a rotating private
  address. A phone forgotten in Settings and paired again comes back under a new address, so BondKeeper read the
  old address's absence as "the stack erased the bond", rebooted ~3 s after the successful pairing and wrote the
  OLD keys back over the new pairing. The phone's new key then failed ("Failed to encrypt…"), the stack erased the
  restored bond, heal 2, heal 3, give up: the board keyless, the app on "keys out of sync". Every forget + re-pair
  went round the same loop.

- [x] **1. A successful pairing is the truth; only a failed encryption heals.** `bonds::next_step` (BondStore.h):
  after a success the copy follows the stack's store even when a kept address is gone; heal only when the latest
  auth failed. Firmware 1.3.
